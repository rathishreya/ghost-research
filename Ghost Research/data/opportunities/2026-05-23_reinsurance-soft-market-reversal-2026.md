## What the report would cover
- A line-by-line and region-by-region softening map (property-cat vs retro vs casualty; US vs Europe vs Asia) quantifying Jan-1 moves and projecting mid-year.
- The capital-glut mechanics: how much of the softening is traditional retained earnings vs new ILS/cat-bond inflows, and how durable each is.
- Where attachment points and terms are holding firm vs eroding — the structural discipline that survived the last hard market and where it's now slipping.
- A contrarian risk flag: which softening lines are pricing below modeled loss cost given climate-loss trend, i.e. where the cycle is overshooting.
- Mid-year renewal playbook for cedents (how much rate relief to push for) and for ILS investors (where spreads no longer compensate).

## Who would buy it
Reinsurance buyers and CUOs need to know how aggressively to negotiate mid-year renewals; ILS/cat-bond PMs need to know where spreads have compressed past fair value; insurance equity analysts need the cycle-turn read for their models. The contrarian "where the market is mispricing risk" angle is exactly the cross-source pattern detection a buyer can't assemble from a single broker's renewal note — easily worth $500 against a single placement or trade.
