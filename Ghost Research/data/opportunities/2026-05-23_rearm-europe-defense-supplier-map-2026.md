## What the report would cover
- A breakdown of the €800B / SAFE €150B by Capability Coalition (air & missile defense, artillery, munitions, drones, etc.) and by member-state national plan.
- Where orders are concentrating among primes (Rheinmetall, Thales, Naval Group, Dassault, Nammo) and which tier-2/3 suppliers sit on the critical path.
- The industrial-capacity reality check: where order books already exceed achievable production, creating delivery risk and sub-supplier bottlenecks (energetics, propellants, machine tools).
- The US-exclusion dynamic: which categories favor European-only suppliers and where US primes can still participate via local content.
- An investor/supplier positioning matrix: where capacity expansion (not just order growth) creates durable winners.
