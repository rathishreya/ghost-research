## What the report would cover
- A single consolidated obligations matrix across OCC, FDIC, Federal Reserve and Treasury rules: who must comply with what, by when.
- Reserve-asset, 2-business-day redemption, capital, and custody standards reconciled across the agencies (where they align vs diverge).
- The BSA/AML and sanctions-program build that PPSIs now owe as "financial institutions," with a gap-analysis template.
- The foreign-issuer pathway: what non-US stablecoin issuers must do to keep serving US users, and the prohibition that blocks everyone else.
- A 120-day countdown readiness checklist keyed to the likely July 2026 final-rule date.

Payments and digital-asset leads, GCs, and CCOs at banks, fintechs and stablecoin issuers must turn four overlapping rulemakings into one compliant operating model before the effective date — reconciling them is precisely the cross-source legal/regulatory synthesis that's painful to do in-house. A $500 consolidated map saves weeks of outside-counsel time and de-risks US market access.
