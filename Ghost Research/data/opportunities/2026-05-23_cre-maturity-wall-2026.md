## What the report would cover
- A market-by-market map of 2026-maturing CRE debt segmented by asset class (office vs multifamily vs industrial) and by "has a refi path / underwater / no path."
- Quantification of the "extend-and-pretend" overhang: how much modified/extended debt from 2024–25 is now stacked into the 2026 window.
- Cap-rate and value-decline deltas by metro that determine whether a refi covers the maturing balance — the loan-to-value gap owners must plug with fresh equity.
- Where distressed-buying opportunity is concentrated (multifamily vs office) and the realistic recovery-rate ranges special servicers are working to.
- A 90-day watchlist of submarkets most likely to tip from "extend" to "fire sale."

## Who would buy it
CRE debt funds, PE real-estate acquisitions teams, and special-servicing leads are all making extend-vs-foreclose-vs-buy decisions right now with incomplete, broker-fragmented data. A $500 synthesis that maps where the refi path actually breaks — instead of generic "there's a wall coming" commentary — directly de-risks a single deal worth millions, making the price trivially justifiable.
