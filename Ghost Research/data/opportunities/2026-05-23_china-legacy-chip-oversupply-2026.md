## What the report would cover
- A node-by-node map of where Chinese mature-node overcapacity lands (power, analog, microcontrollers, legacy logic) and projected price erosion.
- The subsidy and dumping mechanics: how $150B+ of state support translates into below-cost trailing-edge supply.
- Western dependency exposure: which auto and industrial OEMs and which Western fabs are most reliant on the categories China is flooding.
- The export-control / trade-action overlay: how the Jan-2026 BIS shift and pending legacy-chip tariffs change sourcing calculus.
- A procurement risk matrix: where to de-risk single-source dependency vs where Chinese supply is now structurally cheapest.

## Who would buy it
Semiconductor and industrial/auto procurement teams, semi investors, and export-control counsel must decide sourcing and pricing strategy against a flood of subsidized legacy chips colliding with shifting trade rules. Mapping overcapacity to specific category price erosion and dependency exposure is a multi-source synthesis (capacity data + trade policy + demand) that's hard to Google — directly informing sourcing and investment decisions worth far more than $500.
