## What the report would cover
- A per-vessel-class (tanker, bulker, container, GT bands) compliance-cost model under both the Base and Direct-Compliance GFI targets through 2030/2035.
- How the Remedial-Unit pricing mechanism and the IMO Net-Zero Fund work in practice, and the likely cost-per-tonne range.
- Alternative-fuel cost curves (biofuels, methanol, ammonia, LNG transition) and which pathway minimizes lifetime compliance cost by trade lane.
- The well-to-wake accounting twist: how lifecycle emissions change which "green" fuels actually qualify for rewards vs penalties.
- A decision framework for owners (retrofit vs newbuild vs charter-out) and charterers/traders (how the cost passes through to freight).

Shipowner and charterer strategy heads, bunkering procurement, and shipping-finance investors must make vessel, fuel, and charter decisions now against a pricing mechanism adopting in October 2026.
