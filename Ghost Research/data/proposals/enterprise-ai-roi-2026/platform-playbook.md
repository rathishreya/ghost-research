## 1. LinkedIn — the workhorse · 55% of spend ($275)

**Best ad format:** LinkedIn Sponsored Content — single image + single document ad (carousel). Skip video ads here; LinkedIn video CPM is too high for our cap and the visual codes we ship work harder as stills on the LI feed.

**Creative direction:** Three of our lanes ladder onto LinkedIn — `cinematic-ink` for the hero stops, `methodology-specimen` for the authority moments, `editorial-paper` for the confrontation. We deliberately mix lanes across the 14 days so the audience never sees "another navy ad" — the variance breaks ad blindness while every variant remains brand-locked.

**Headlines (rotate one per ad slot):**
- "1,000 GenAI deployments. 4 survived diligence."
- "Everyone bought AI. Almost nobody priced it correctly."
- "Enterprise AI. Audited."
- "The receipts are in."

**Hooks (in-post text — first 140 chars matter most on LinkedIn truncation):**
- *Cold:* "We audited 1,047 enterprise AI deployments against PE-grade diligence. The receipts are not what your vendor decks claim."
- *Authority:* "We pulled § 1.2 of our methodology so you can judge the work before you read the brief. Inclusion rules below."
- *Confrontation:* "If your board is asking what the AI line item delivered, you have until Q2. The audit publishes Monday."

**CTAs:**
- Week 1: `Get on the list →`
- Week 2: `Read the brief — $500 →`

**Scene breakdown — Concept 01 (hero, 1:1):**
- Frame opens with deep navy-to-black gradient, single red specular highlight bleeding in from upper-right (mimics Linear's product render edge light)
- Top-left: `Ghost Research.` wordmark (red dot)
- Top-right: caps-locked metadata `CASPR · 2026 Q3 AUDIT / DROPS JUNE 9, 2026`
- Center-left: Oranienbaum headline `1,000 GenAI deployments.` with italic-red emphasis `4 survived diligence.`
- Below headline: 56px red rule + Manrope sub `1M+ curated sources. 32 named SMEs. Every claim cited.`
- Bottom-left: `Self-serve · drops June 9.` ($500 anchor — not present W1)
- Bottom-right: Manrope-bold red `Get on the list →`

**Visual style:** Cinematic-ink. The frame breathes. 70%+ negative space. One hero line. One accent rule. One CTA. **No stock photos. No "AI illustration." No people in W1 hero work.**

**Retention strategy (per ad):** Stop the scroll in 0.3s with the hero line, hold the eye on the rule (the eye naturally rests on the red mark), deliver the credibility sub, exit on the CTA. We never give the eye a second focal element — every frame is a single thought.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **Confidence signal:** No-button-just-arrow CTA reads as "we don't need to push you." Linear/Apple/Stripe all use this. The lack of a button is the status signal.
- **Price anchor:** $500 appears on W2 ads not as a button label but as a sentence ("Read the brief — $500"). Reframes the price as a value claim, not a transaction friction.
- **Specificity over breadth:** "1,047 deployments" lands harder than "thousands of deployments." Real numbers signal real work.
- **Authority through restraint:** Saying *less* than your competitors signals you have more than them.

**Posting strategy:**
- **Day 1, 3, 5, 7:** post organically to Joy Sharma's personal page → boost the high-performer with the W1 hype ad.
- **Day 8:** dedicated launch sponsored slot, $80 budget — the biggest single-day spend of the campaign.
- **Day 11:** carousel ad — load Concept 11 + Concept 10 (TOC) + Concept 13 (close) as three slides → drive longer dwell + higher click-through.
- **Cadence:** one Sponsored impression per user per 18 hours. Frequency cap = 4 across the 14 days.
- **Targeting layers:** Sales Navigator-style filters → titles {CFO, VP Finance, Head of Strategy, PE Associate, Strategy Consultant, Head of AI, CIO}, companies $1B+ revenue, US/UK/EU, 1st-degree connections to anyone in Ghost Research's network.

## 2. X (formerly Twitter) — the AI-finance crowd · 30% of spend ($150)

**Best ad format:** Promoted single-image post (1:1 or 4:5). Skip video promos on X; bot/algo noise reduces value. Boost organic threads.

**Creative direction:** X rewards the **methodology-specimen** and **editorial-paper** lanes. AI Twitter is a chronically skeptical audience — they reward depth of citation and confrontational framing, and they hate corporate gloss. Our `concept-02-x-methodology` page reads exactly like a screenshot of a published academic paper, which is the visual code they trust.

**Headlines + hooks (the tweet text matters more than the image on X):**
- *Cold (Day 2 promoted):* "We audited 1,047 enterprise GenAI deployments against PE-grade diligence rules. Four survived. The audit publishes June 9. Methodology page attached."
- *Authority (Day 5 promoted):* "What does $500 of institutional research look like in 2026? Specs ↓"
- *Confrontation (Day 13 promoted):* "What did the $300B in enterprise AI spend actually deliver? Read the audit — $500."

**CTAs:** Embedded as the last line of the tweet (no button on Promoted text + image): `Read the audit — $500 → read.ghostresearch.com/ai-roi-2026`

**Image/video concepts to use:**

| Day | File | Why on X |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | `concept-02-w1d2-x-methodology.png` | Looks like a published paper — AI Twitter shares it as "this is interesting." |
| 5 | `concept-05-w1d5-x-specs.png` | Stripe-press spec card — earns engagement from product Twitter. |
| 10 | `concept-10-w2d10-toc.png` | Table-of-contents reveal — drives bookmark/save action. |
| 13 | `concept-13-w2d13-close.png` | Confrontational close with the four key numbers. |

**Scene breakdown — Concept 02 (X methodology page, 1:1):**
- White paper background, off-white `#F8F8FF`
- Top rule with Manrope caps `CASPR · 2026 Q3 · ENTERPRISE AI ROI AUDIT` + Oranienbaum page number `— p. 03 —`
- Two columns of Newsreader serif justified body type
- Left column: § 1.2 Inclusion rules + § 1.3 Sample composition + tiny footnote `[3] See Appendix A …`
- Right column: huge Oranienbaum pullquote on left red rule — "Only *four* of the 1,047 deployments survived a standard PE diligence protocol when payback was tested against pre-deployment baseline."
- Below pullquote: § 1.4 Failure typology
- Footer rule with Ghost wordmark left, red `Drops June 9 · get on the list →` right
- **The trick:** this reads as a screenshot of the actual report. Users *bookmark* it. Bookmarks beat likes on X for downstream conversion.

**Visual style:** Methodology-specimen. The work IS the ad. Restraint communicates "we have more where this came from."

**Retention strategy:** First 140 chars of the tweet ("We audited 1,047 enterprise GenAI deployments…") stops the scroll. The methodology screenshot earns the 5-second hold. The embedded URL at the end converts the user who's already decided.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **Bookmarkable:** X power users save ads that *look* like reference material. Bookmarks → return visits → purchase.
- **Quoted-RT bait:** AI/finance Twitter loves quote-tweeting numbers ("$0.93 ROI?? lol"). Every QRT is free distribution to a buyer-adjacent audience.
- **The screenshot effect:** Users believe a methodology page they screenshot more than a Buy Now button. We give them the screenshot.

**Posting strategy:**
- **Promote Joy Sharma's organic tweets** — she posts a Day-2 thread (4 tweets: hook + screenshot + finding + CTA). The third tweet in the thread gets promoted.
- **Day 5:** post spec card image + tweet "What $500 of institutional research looks like in 2026 ↓"
- **Day 10:** post the TOC + line "If you've been waiting for the audit — here's what's inside."
- **Day 13:** post Concept 13 + line "What did the $300B actually deliver? ↓" + URL.
- **Frequency cap:** 3 per user per 14 days. No more.
- **Targeting layers:** keyword interest {enterprise AI, AI ROI, due diligence, CFO, PE associate, AI investment}, follower-of {a16z, Ben Thompson, Stratechery, The Information, Matt Levine}, geo US/UK/EU.

## 3. Meta (Instagram + Facebook) — Reels + retargeting · 15% of spend ($75)

**Best ad format:** Reels (9:16 video) + 4:5 feed static for retargeting. **No Stories static** (audience scrolls past too fast), **no carousel** (better on LinkedIn).

**Creative direction:** Meta is where Linear-grade `cinematic-ink` motion converts. The cinematic 8-second reveal video is the most platform-native asset we have — it competes with Reels content visually, not with ads. The Pollinations strategist photo with Ken-Burns + burned-in $500 CTA is the closer for retargeting.

**Headlines + hooks (in-post caption, first 80 chars matter):**
- *Cold (Day 6 Reels boost):* "1,047 enterprise AI deployments. Four survived diligence. The audit drops Monday."
- *Launch (Day 9 Reels):* "What did the $300B in enterprise AI actually deliver? Read the audit — $500."
- *Retargeting (Day 12):* "If you've been watching the AI ROI conversation, this is the audit."

**CTAs:**
- Reels CTA sticker: `Read the brief` (button) → landing page (Week 2 only)
- In-caption: `→ read.ghostresearch.com/ai-roi-2026 — $500`

**Image/video concepts to use:**

| Day | File | Format | Why on Meta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | `concept-06-w1d6-reels-teaser.mp4` (8s) | 9:16 video | W1 cinematic teaser — Apple-keynote pacing. |
| 9 | `concept-09-w2d9-launch-reels.mp4` (8s) | 9:16 video | W2 conversion push — same template, $500 anchor. |
| 12 | `concept-12-w2d12-analyst-motion.mp4` (8s) | 9:16 video Ken-Burns | Retargeting closer — human face = trust. |

**Scene breakdown — Concept 09 (launch Reels, 9:16, 8s):**

| t | Visual | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0s | Black frame. Wordmark + pretitle `Now live · Caspr Self-Serve · $500` fade-up | Identity |
| 1.6s | Hero number `$0.93` rises in (slow opacity + 20px translate-y). Red bloom blossoms under it | The shock — average ROI per AI dollar |
| 3.2s | Red rule extends from 0 to 80px + caps `AVERAGE ROI PER AI DOLLAR` pin appears | The frame |
| 4.0s | Editorial line rises: "Across 1,047 audited enterprise deployments. *Read the receipts.*" | Context |
| 5.4s | CTA `Read the brief — $500 →` + spec line fade in | The offer |
| 6.0–8.0s | Hold + breathe before loop | Dwell |

**Music note:** sustained synth pad starting at 0s (calm authority). Soft sub-bass hit at 1.6s when the number lands. Hi-hat tick at 3.2s when the rule extends. Last beat held under CTA. Free royalty-free options: search "calm corporate suspense" on Pixabay; trim to 8s with ffmpeg.

**Visual style:** Cinematic-ink. Pure deep gradient + serif typography + red accent. **Motion philosophy: opacity + 6px translate-y, never bounces or slides.** Apple-keynote ease — Linear-grade restraint.

**Retention strategy:**
- **0–2s rule (Reels):** the wordmark + pretitle text gives the algorithm a clear signal of who this is for ("$500" eyebrow flags qualified spenders → algorithm pushes to similar audiences).
- **Stop-frame at 1.6s:** the hero number "$0.93" is the thumbnail-grab. Users who see it pause-scroll.
- **No talking head, no sound dependency** — the entire ad works on mute (which is how 85% of Reels are consumed).
- **Loop-friendly:** the final 2s holds clean so the video loops without a hard cut.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **The "what did I just see" effect:** the audit-grade specificity (0.93, 1,047, 4 of 1,047) doesn't match the visual code most viewers associate with marketing. Their brain pauses to reconcile = scroll-stop.
- **No "Buy Now" button on the asset itself.** The CTA is part of the type composition. The Reels CTA sticker on the platform does the conversion work — we don't compete with it.
- **The $500 mention in the pretitle** pre-qualifies viewers — anyone who scrolls past on price has self-rejected, the remaining audience is warmer.

**Posting strategy:**
- **Day 6 (Sat):** boost the W1 teaser Reels as organic post to Joy Sharma's IG + Ghost Research page → $20 boost, target lookalike to LI page.
- **Day 9 (Tue):** launch Reels boosted with Meta Ads Manager → $35 budget, audience = LinkedIn page-visitors uploaded via Custom Audience.
- **Day 12 (Fri):** retargeting Reels (Concept 12) → $20 budget, audience = anyone who hit landing page in W1 but didn't buy.
- **Frequency cap:** 4 per user across 14 days.
- **Placement:** Reels + Stories only. No Feed (CTR is half), no Right Column (waste).
- **Time of day:** schedule for 7–9am local in target geo — when board-deck-builders are commuting / pre-meeting scrolling.

## 4. Cross-platform retargeting layer (overlay on all three)

**Audience:**
- Landing-page-visitors who did NOT convert in W1 → retarget W2 with Concept 11 (stat reveal) + Concept 12 (analyst Reels).
- Anyone who converted on the audit → exclude from W2 ad targeting (waste) + push Ghost Elite cross-sell ad ("Want a custom audit of YOUR stack? Talk to us.").

**Budget reserved:** $50 of the $500 cap, spread across LinkedIn ($25) + Meta ($25). No X retargeting (custom-audience matching on X is too noisy at this scale).

## 6. What to actually do tonight before launch (Day -3 / -2 / -1 prep)
