## 1. The strategic conviction

The market has spent two years buying enterprise GenAI on vendor decks. **Boards are now demanding receipts.** The CFO who signed off on the AI initiative in 2024 has to justify the line item in the 2026 Q2 board pack — without the receipts, the budget gets cut. **This audit is the receipts.** Ghost Research wins by being the first credible institutional source to publish a baseline-adjusted, PE-grade audit of what 1,000 enterprise GenAI deployments actually delivered.

**Lead angle:** post-hype. We do not argue against AI — we *audit* it. The tone is the doctor with the X-ray, not the investor with the slogan.
