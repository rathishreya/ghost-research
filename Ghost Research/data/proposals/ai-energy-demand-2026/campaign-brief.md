**Hook 1 — [Named villain] — WEEK 1 SAFE**
- *"$147B in announced hyperscaler capex just landed on 9 utility balance sheets. The grid isn't ready. The atlas drops June 1."*
- **Why it works:** Names the villain (the capex number, the 9 utilities) without revealing which 9. Creates the canonical gap: *I need to know which 9*. Energy PMs and utility analysts will read past the headline.

**Hook 2 — [Insider POV] — WEEK 1 SAFE**

Reader cannot satisfy the gap without the report. Speaks the audience's exact language (PJM nodes, stress-test) — outsiders self-exclude.

**Hook 3 — [Specificity over category] — WEEK 2 ONLY (content-active)**
