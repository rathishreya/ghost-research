# Visual Concepts — The AI Energy Demand Atlas 2026

## Strategy summary

Every concept is built on a **specificity-of-prop** principle: each frame must contain at least one element that *only* an energy/utility/hyperscale buyer would recognize — a substation transformer, a PJM load-map screen, a printed utility 10-Q with marked pages, a grid-interconnect queue printout, a cooling tower at dusk, a control room. **The swap test passes for every concept**: replace "AI Energy Demand Atlas" with "Music Economy" or "CFO Defense Pack" and the visual breaks. That is the bar.

**Theme mix:** 10 dark-theme (deep indigo dusk grade — the "trading floor at sundown" register) + **2 LIGHT-THEME concepts** (Day 6 publishing-anticipation card and Day 9 stat-reveal). Light theme breaks visual pattern at the moment of the launch, signaling premium-consultancy register vs. urgent-trading register.

**Copy mechanism map (each headline tagged with its info-gap mechanism):** Named villain → 4 concepts. Insider POV → 3. Specificity over category → 3. Time-decay urgency → 1. Hidden cost → 1.
