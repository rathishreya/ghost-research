## SECTION 1: CAMPAIGN FOUNDATION

**The one insight driving this campaign:** Every cross-border seller knows the rules are changing on 1 July — almost none of them have re-run their unit economics at the new landed cost, by SKU and by lane. The gap isn't *awareness*; it's *a model they can act on*.

**Positioning for ads:** The institutional-grade landed-cost model the operators have been arguing about in Slack but nobody has actually built — duty + per-parcel fee + fulfillment shift, per SKU, per lane.

**Competitive gap this campaign owns:** Customs bulletins explain the *rule*. Law firms explain the *risk*. No one publishes the *math* — the per-SKU, per-lane margin reset and the Shein/Temu competitive-response map in one place. Ghost owns "the number," not "the news."

**The offer:** One repricing decision informed by this model protects more margin than the report costs many times over — and the alternative is repricing 10,000 SKUs in the dark with two weeks on the clock. (Per brand rule, price is never named in ad copy; it reveals on the landing page.)
