# Campaign brief — Supply chain AI forecast accuracy (2026)

**Slug:** `supply-chain-ai-forecast`
**Product:** Caspr. Self-Serve report (~$500)
**Publish date:** 2026-06-01
**Geography:** US + UK + EU (English-only creative)
**Flight length:** 14 days, Week 1 = hype, Week 2 = sales
**Budget:** $500 hard cap (off-the-shelf)
**Author:** Ghost Research / Caspr.ai, founded by Joy Sharma (ex-McKinsey)

---

## 1. Who we're talking to

Three buyer profiles, in priority order:

| # | Buyer | Pain | Trigger to click |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Head of S&OP / Supply Chain at a $1B+ industrial or CPG** | Their ERP-fed planning model is 4–8 weeks lagged. They get blamed when forecast misses cause inventory write-downs. | "Decision next month, dashboard from last quarter" |
| 2 | **PE / M&A diligence lead, industrials desk** | They have to sign off on supply-chain assumptions in 5-day diligence sprints. Need a defensible third-party reference. | "Boardroom edition", "expert-vetted" |
| 3 | **Senior strategy consultant (Big-4 alum, boutique partner)** | Sells supply-chain advisory; needs a fast credible synthesis to anchor proposals. | "1M+ curated sources", "every claim cited" |

Geographies are weighted by industrial GDP exposure: **US 50% / UK 20% / EU 30%**. Do not run in IN/ME on this flight — buyer concentration is not there for supply-chain content yet.

## 8. Kill switch

Trigger any of these → pause all non-winning ads + escalate:

1. Day 5: zero waitlist conversions and CTR < 0.25% on **all** Week 1 hooks.
2. Day 10: zero purchases and ROAS < 0.5x.
3. Day 7: landing page bounce > 75% across all UTMs (= page problem, not ad problem).
4. Brand-safety incident (any ad flagged by platform review).

If kill-switch triggers on day 5–7: write `lessons.md` documenting **which hook failed and why**, push the surviving hook to 100% spend, and let it finish the flight on one creative line rather than the planned three.
