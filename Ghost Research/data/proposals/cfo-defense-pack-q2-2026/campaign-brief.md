## 1. The strategic conviction

Every CFO of a $1B+ enterprise has a Q2 board meeting where they will be asked
to defend the AI line item from the 2024-25 spend cycle. The 2026 board cycle
is **the receipts cycle**. Without a defensible third-party audit, the AI
budget gets cut and the CFO loses credibility. **This audit is their survival
kit.** Ghost Research wins by being first to market with the institutional
PE-grade audit that *names* what worked, names what didn't, and gives the CFO
the exact answers their board will demand.

**Reframe shift from last campaign:** same underlying data (the 1,047 audit)
but reframed from the *researcher's* perspective ("here's an audit") to the
*buyer's* perspective ("here's your survival kit for Q2"). Identity-led,
emotionally engineered — the buyer doesn't buy the audit, they buy *being the
CFO who walks into Q2 with answers.*

**Visual law (single, non-negotiable):** every ad = cinematic Pollinations
photo + dark gradient scrim + Oranienbaum headline overlay + Manrope sub +
red CTA pill. **No type-only ads. No white backgrounds. No academic mockups.
No bare photos.** This is what last campaign got wrong.
