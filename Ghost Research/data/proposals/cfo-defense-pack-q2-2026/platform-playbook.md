## 🔵 LinkedIn — workhorse · $275 · 55%

**Best ad format:** Sponsored Content single image (1:1 + 4:5) + one Sponsored Reels video on Day 6 + Sponsored Document carousel on Day 4 (concept C04 + C05 as a 2-slide bundle).

**Creative direction:** Every Sponsored ad uses the same visual law — Pollinations photo + dark scrim + Oranienbaum overlay + red CTA — but the **photographic scene rotates daily** so the feed never sees "another navy ad." Rotation: dawn boardroom CFO (C01/C07) → late-night window silhouette (C02) → empty boardroom with single key light (C03/C13) → hands on sensitivity chart (C04) → CFO at desk pre-prep (C05) → hero finale shot (C08/C14). Variance is photographic, not stylistic.

**Headlines (rotated):**
- "What your board will ask. *What you'll say.*"
- "The Q2 board pack just got *harder.*"
- "Publishes *Monday.*"
- "Eight of 1,047 *survived.*"
- "$0.74. *Per AI dollar.*"

**Hooks (in-post first 140 chars):**
- *Cold (Day 1):* "Every CFO of a $1B+ enterprise has a Q2 board meeting where they will be asked to defend the AI line item. This is the audit they'll use."
- *Authority (Day 4):* "We pulled § 1.2 of the audit's inclusion rules so you can judge the work before you read the brief."
- *Confrontation (Day 11):* "$0.74 average ROI per AI dollar. Across 1,047 audited enterprise deployments. Be ready when your board asks."

**CTAs:**
- Week 1: `Get on the list →`, `Reserve early access →`
- Week 2: `Read the pack — $500 →`

**Scene breakdown — Concept C01 (Day 1 LinkedIn hero, 1:1):**
- Background: a senior CFO in his fifties seated alone at the head of a polished walnut conference table at dawn, single window light camera-right showing blue-hour skyline, single brass desk lamp warm accent in background. Bloomberg Originals cinematography. Photoreal, 50mm prime, shallow DOF.
- Top-left: small `Ghost Research.` wordmark with red dot. Top-right: small caps `CASPR · 2026 Q3 / DROPS JUNE 9, 2026`.
- Bottom 40% of frame: dark gradient scrim (rgba(6,6,45,0.45) → 0.95) for legibility.
- Over the scrim, lower-left:
  - Red pretitle caps: `— THE CFO DEFENSE PACK —`
  - Oranienbaum headline 84px: `What your board will ask. ` + italic red `What you'll say.`
  - Red 56px rule
  - Manrope sub: "The audit of 1,047 enterprise AI deployments. 32 named SMEs. Every claim cited. Twelve days to your Q2 board."
- Bottom right: red CTA pill `Get on the list →`

**Visual style:** Cinematic photographic. Dark navy + cool tungsten warmth + single accent red. No flat-color zones. No type-only ads. Negative space is the gradient scrim where the photo darkens — not blank.

**Retention strategy (per ad):**
- 0.3s — the photographic scene stops the scroll. A real-looking senior CFO at dawn reads as "Bloomberg documentary," not "marketing." Pause.
- 0.6s — eye lands on the italic-red emphasis word (the only red text in headline).
- 1.2s — sub builds in the credibility (1,047 / 32 / every claim cited).
- 1.8s — CTA pill on the lower-right; eye exits to click.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **Identity capture:** "What your board will ask" frames the buyer as the one who'll be asked — they become the protagonist of the ad. They're not buying a report; they're buying *being a CFO who has answers.*
- **Price absent in W1, anchored in W2:** the W1 ads sell the *waitlist*, not the product. W2 ads attach $500 to every CTA + every eyebrow. This is how you sell a $500 product without seeming desperate.
- **Italic-red emphasis word is the gut-punch:** every headline has ONE italic-red word/phrase that carries the emotional weight. "What you'll *say.*" / "*are in.*" / "*survived.*" / "Per AI *dollar.*"

**Posting strategy:**
- **Joy posts organically** on Days 1/3/5/7 (one variant per post). The W1 ads that come from Joy's personal account outperform brand-page ads ~2x on LinkedIn.
- **Day 4:** publish as Sponsored Document carousel (C04 + C05 + C03 as 3 slides). Carousels triple dwell time.
- **Day 8 (LAUNCH):** $80 single-day spend on C08. Biggest day of the flight.
- **Day 11:** C11 ($0.74 stat reveal) — second highest-spend day after launch ($35).
- **Frequency cap:** 4 per user per 14 days.
- **Targeting:** Sales-Nav-style {CFO, VP Finance, Controller, Head of Strategy, PE Associate, Strategy Consultant, CIO, Head of AI} × $1B+ revenue companies × US/UK/EU. Layer 1st-degree connections to Ghost Research's network.

## ⚫ X — AI/finance crowd · $125 · 25%

**Best ad format:** Promoted single image (1:1). Skip video on X (bot noise dilutes). Most spend goes into boosting Joy's organic threads, where engagement compounds.

**Creative direction:** Same visual law as LinkedIn (photo + scrim + overlay) but lean into the empty-boardroom and dark-window-silhouette scenes (C03, C13, C14). These feel like film stills, not ads. AI/finance Twitter rewards "what is this even" energy — and a dramatic empty boardroom with a single binder under a key light delivers that.

**Headlines + hooks (tweet text matters more than image on X):**
- *Day 3 promoted:* "We audited 1,047 enterprise AI deployments against PE-grade diligence. Eight survived. Audit publishes Monday." + screenshot of C03.
- *Day 7 promoted thread:* (3-tweet thread by Joy) tweet 1 (hook), tweet 2 (photo C07), tweet 3 (URL). Promote tweet 2.
- *Day 13 promoted:* "What does $500 of institutional research buy a CFO in 2026? The audit of 1,047 deployments. Eight survived. ↓"

**CTAs:** Last line of every promoted tweet: `→ read.ghostresearch.com/cfo-defense-pack — $500`

**Scene breakdown — Concept C03 (Day 3 X promoted, 1:1):**
- Background: empty premium boardroom at dusk, single closed leather binder placed precisely on a polished walnut table, single dramatic overhead key light creating long shadows.
- Bottom half: dark gradient scrim.
- Pretitle: red `— THE AUDIT —`
- Oranienbaum headline 108px: `Publishes ` + italic red `Monday.`
- Red rule + Manrope sub: "1,047 enterprise AI deployments. Eight survived a standard PE diligence pass. Every claim cited."
- Bottom-right: red CTA pill `Get on the list →`

**Visual style:** Photographic cinematic film-still. The empty-boardroom scene is the secret weapon for X — it's neither a person photo nor a stat card, so the audience can't pre-judge it as either marketing or journalism. They linger.

**Retention strategy:** First 140 chars of tweet stops the scroll. The photographic film-still earns a 4–6 second hold (which is huge on X). URL in the last line converts.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **Bookmark-bait:** AI Twitter saves "what is this even" content. Bookmarks beat likes for downstream conversion 4:1.
- **QRT-bait:** the $0.74 ROI stat in concept C11 / the C13 confrontation will get QRT'd by AI skeptics ("$0.74?? lol"). Every QRT = free distribution to adjacent buyer-pool.
- **Authority through restraint:** the empty-boardroom-with-binder scene says *we don't need to put a face on this* — the institution is the proof.

**Posting strategy:**
- **Joy posts a 4-tweet thread Day 2** (hook → screenshot → finding → CTA). Promote tweet 2 ($25 budget).
- **Day 7:** Joy posts C07 organically + promoted ($25).
- **Day 13:** Joy posts C13 confrontation + promoted ($30).
- **Day 14:** Joy posts C14 (finale) + promoted ($20). Last call.
- **Cross-promotion:** $25 reserved for retweeting / amplifying a known AI-Twitter voice's quote of the audit (if it earns one organically by Day 10).
- **Frequency cap:** 3 per user across 14 days.
- **Targeting:** keyword interest {enterprise AI, AI ROI, CFO, PE diligence, AI investment} × followers-of {Stratechery, Matt Levine, Ben Thompson, The Information, a16z}. Geo US/UK/EU.

## 🟣 Meta (IG + FB) — Reels + retargeting · $100 · 20%

**Best ad format:** Reels (9:16 video) + 4:5 feed retargeting. No Stories static (audience scrolls past). No carousels (LinkedIn does that better).

**Creative direction:** This is where the cinematic-photographic motion does its heaviest lifting. The 8-second Reels videos (C06 W1 teaser, C09 W2 launch, C12 retargeting) all combine real Pollinations photography with motion + brand overlay. **All three videos use the photo as the literal background of the kinetic** — no flat color anywhere.

**Headlines + hooks (in-post caption, first 80 chars matter):**

- *Day 9 launch Reels:* "$0.74 per AI dollar. Across 1,047 audited deployments. Be ready when your board asks."
- *Day 12 retargeting:* "If you've been watching the AI ROI conversation, this is the audit. — $500."

**CTAs:**

**Music note:** Calm-corporate-tension synth pad starts at 0s. Soft sub-bass hit at 2.0s when `$0.74` lands. Free royalty-free options at Pixabay/Bensound; trim to 8s with ffmpeg.

**Visual style:** Cinematic photographic motion. Every video frame has a real photographic background — not flat color. The photo IS the canvas, the type IS the message.

**Retention strategy:**
- **0–0.5s scroll-stop:** the silhouette boardroom + `NOW LIVE · $500` eyebrow signals luxury-tech instantly. The Reels algorithm reads the early hold-rate and pushes to lookalike audience.
- **2.0s hero number reveal:** the `$0.74` is the thumbnail-frame for anyone who pause-scrolls. Below-break-even ROI is the kind of stat that demands a second look.
- **No talking head, no sound dependency:** the video works on mute (85% of Reels viewing). All meaning conveyed visually.
- **Loop-clean final 2s:** the CTA holds clean so the video loops without a hard cut. Loop count = algorithm boost.

**Conversion psychology:**
- **Premium aesthetic = price tolerance:** the cinematic photography + restrained motion signal a $500 product before the price ever appears. Viewer's brain is already pricing the offer up — when $500 shows, it feels like a bargain.
- **The $500 in pretitle pre-qualifies:** anyone scrolling past on price has self-rejected, the remaining viewers are warmer.
- **No "Buy Now" button on the asset itself.** Reels CTA sticker on the platform does the conversion work — we don't compete with it. The asset just builds the desire.

**Posting strategy:**
- **Day 6 (Sat):** Boost C06 Reels organic on Joy IG + Ghost Research page. $25 boost, audience = LI page lookalike via Custom Audience.
- **Day 9 (Tue):** C09 launch Reels boosted via Meta Ads Manager. $40 budget. Audience = LP visitors uploaded from W1 + LI page lookalike.
- **Day 12 (Fri):** C12 retargeting Reels. $35 budget. Audience = anyone who hit LP in W1 + did not buy.
- **Frequency cap:** 4 per user across 14 days.
- **Placement:** Reels + Stories only. Skip Feed (CTR halves) and Right Column (waste).
- **Schedule for 7–9am local** in target geography — board-pack-prep hours.
